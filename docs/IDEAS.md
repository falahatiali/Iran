# Ideas — not yet scheduled

Bold product ideas recorded during daily improvement runs. None of these are promises; none may ship without sources.

## 2026-10-29

- **Tablet theatre:** a single-tablet stage that shows one Hallock category at a time as a clay “card” (category letter, PF range, verified fact, citation) with keyboard next/previous — a quieter sibling of the ration explorer, not a fake cuneiform renderer.
- **Travel map of authorizations:** plot only satrapal origins Iranica actually names (Sardis / Artaphernes, plus “previously unknown” governors as a labelled unknown set). No invented roads.
- **Women’s ration duet:** two columns — Hallock’s L 1–3 remark (women more numerous; some paid above men) beside category N (mothers of boys vs girls) — with the philosophical layer asking about the fruit of labour without attributing feminism to Darius.
- **Cite-pack download:** one `.bib` / CSL-JSON file generated from the `bibliography` table for the current page, so a student can drop Hallock 1969 and Iranica 2017 into Zotero without scraping HTML.
- **Passage commentary as a radio play (audio):** Persian and English readings of *only* Rogers 1912 public-domain lines, with a spoken “this is not the British Museum translation” disclaimer.
- **Murashu next:** the Babylonian private-business archive named in the philosophy directive, as the third research track — land, credit, and whether “private” there means anything like individual title. Do not start until a citable edition is in hand.
- **Behistun as the next inscription object:** reuse the Cylinder content model (passages, layers, cite/share) for DB/OP/Elamite columns, with the same ban on invented signs in 3D.
- **Disputed-number widget:** a small UI that shows Herzfeld’s 30,000 vs Stolper’s 20–25k side by side, because the interesting fact is the disagreement.
- **PFA Project live shelf:** when a stable OCHRE or ISAC project URL is verified, embed a “what is still unpublished” counter sourced from that page, never guessed.
- **Jannatkhah dossier v1:** only after an ISBN, publisher page, or timestamped transcript exists; until then keep asking his questions without putting words in his mouth.
