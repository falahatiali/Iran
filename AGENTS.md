# Agent instructions

This project has a permanent owner directive:

**[docs/FOUNDATIONAL_PHILOSOPHY.md](docs/FOUNDATIONAL_PHILOSOPHY.md)**

Every change must honor it. In particular:

1. **Three-layer separation, labeled in the UI and in data.** Never mix these as if they were the same kind of claim:
   - **Historical evidence** — what a primary source, museum catalog, or scholarly edition actually establishes.
   - **Philosophical interpretation** — a reading through a theory of individual liberty and property rights.
   - **Modern cultural meaning** — what the object means to Iranians and others today.
2. **Never fabricate citations, quotes, translations, images, or 3D assets.** If a book, scan, license, or edition cannot be verified, record the gap and continue with what is legitimately accessible. Do not present a reconstruction as a museum scan.
3. Individual property rights and liberty are the project's analytical framework. On the Cyrus Cylinder, argue that recognition from the actual wording (return to dwellings, restored cults, lifted yoke, repaired houses, peaceful entry). Do not invent quotes. Do not deny those lines. Keep historical quotation, philosophical interpretation, and modern meaning in separate labeled layers.
4. Keep source text, translations, commentary, and editorial reconstruction as separate fields. Use stable passage IDs across languages. Verify the license of every translation before reproducing it; otherwise summarize and link.
5. Prefer primary evidence, then museums and scholarship. Viral posts are not proof.
